README: Iteration progress

with --max-iterations, the interactive stats line shows completed/max between the elapsed timer and token totals. The count includes finished iterations restored on resume and advances only after the active iteration finishes, whether it succeeds or fails. Runs without an iteration cap omit this segment. To fit crowded stats rows, separator spaces are removed and, if needed, token labels become Σ (total), ↓ (input), and ↑ (output).
